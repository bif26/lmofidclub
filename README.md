## 🎯 About

**Lmofid Club** is a comprehensive e-commerce training course designed by **Simo Life** and **Anas** to guide you through every aspect of building and scaling a successful online store. This course covers everything from foundational e-commerce concepts to advanced marketing strategies.

**Language:** Arabic | **Duration:** 200+ Video Lessons | **Level:** Beginner to Advanced

